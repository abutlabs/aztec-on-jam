Public ledger anyone sees this

What Aztec publishes for each transaction: note hashes (commitments to new private notes), nullifiers (marks of spent notes, not linkable to them), encrypted logs and public state writes. A private payment shows no sender, recipient or amount.

    Aztec's node proposes each checkpoint to JAM instead of Ethereum: the sidecar hands it to the settlement service as a work package; refine verifies it, accumulate records it. Later, Aztec's prover proves a whole epoch and JAM verifies that proof.
