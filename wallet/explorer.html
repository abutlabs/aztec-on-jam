      Checkpoints on JAM

        Epoch proofs on JAM

          What Aztec publishes for each transaction: note hashes (commitments to new private notes), nullifiers (marks of spent notes, not linkable to them), encrypted logs and public state writes. A private payment shows no sender, recipient or amount.

          Recorded on JAM: JAM checked the block's data against its commitments and the proposer's signatures, and holds it in order; nothing yet proves its transactions were executed correctly. Proven on JAM: JAM verified Aztec's epoch proof, one proof that every transaction, block and checkpoint of the epoch is valid.
