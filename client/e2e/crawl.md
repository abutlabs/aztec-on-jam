It is a period of open ledgers. The KYC EMPIRE makes every citizen show their papers,
and writes every payment where any IMPERIAL analyst can read it.

In the distant workshops of AZTEC, rebel engineers have forged a new weapon: the
ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOF, which shows a payment keeps every law while revealing nothing of who
paid whom, or how much.

Now JAM, a world built for any work at all, carries those proofs in its WORK PACKAGES and
checks each one in REFINE, bringing the rebellion the hope of a brighter, freer
galaxy....
