Aztec on JAM

Aztec's private transactions, settled and verified on JAM in Ethereum's place. Aztec's software runs as released; JAM replaces the layer under it.

Aztec

A privacy network. Every transaction is proved on the sender's own device with a zero-knowledge proof, so the chain verifies it without learning sender, receiver or amount. Today it settles on Ethereum.

aztec.network

JAM

Polkadot's next protocol: hundreds of cores of verifiable compute. Services bring their own code; validators execute it, audit each other, and finalize the result.

graypaper.com

This project

Everything Aztec takes from Ethereum — ordering, data availability, proof verification — JAM also provides, with far more verification capacity. We built the bridge, measured every step, and drafted the one protocol addition that makes verification fast.

What stands today

  1. LIVEA working private-payment demo on JAM

    A browser proves a private transfer in about a second; six JAM validators verify it and update encrypted state; the receiver finds the money with their key. Runs on two independent JAM clients.

  2. LIVEAztec v6's own node, settling on JAM

    Aztec's released node proposes its blocks to JAM through our facade. Seven checkpoints settled; no Aztec code patched.

  3. LIVEAztec's proofs, verified by JAM validators

    Aztec's own verifier, compiled for JAM's VM, checked a real Aztec transaction proof on a live six-validator net — practical only with our proposed host calls.

  4. NEXTThe full stack

    Our wallet on Aztec's libraries, real proofs end to end, a prover node. Tracked in #71.
