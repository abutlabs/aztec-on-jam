The demo

Two proofs of concept, each running on a six-validator JAM testnet on one laptop. The first shows private payments in Aztec's model. The second runs Aztec's own software, as released, with JAM in Ethereum's place.

1 · Private payments in Aztec's model

Alice proves a private payment in her browser; JAM's validators verify it and record only ciphertext; Bob finds the money with his key. Our circuit, written to Aztec's design, proved with Aztec's tools.

2 · Aztec's own stack, settling on JAM

The real thing: Aztec's wallet libraries, circuits, sequencer and prover, unchanged. JAM replaces Ethereum underneath. A private token transfer, proved in the browser, verified by Aztec's node, settled and finalized by six JAM validators.

Aztec's, unchanged

  • The PXE, aztec.js and wallet-sdk — the wallet's engine
  • Aztec's circuits: contracts, private kernels, rollup
  • Barretenberg, Aztec's prover
  • Aztec's sequencer node
  • Aztec's prover node, broker and agents

A new Aztec release is a version bump.

Ours

  • The wallet's interface, on Aztec's libraries
  • The JAM sidecar — answers Aztec's node the way Ethereum would, and turns its blocks into JAM work
  • The settlement service — Aztec's Ethereum rollup contract, rewritten as a JAM service
  • lasair, our JAM client, with the JIP built in
